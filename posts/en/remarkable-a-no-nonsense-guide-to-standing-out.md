Hello, wonderful reader! Let’s get straight to the point: being average is so last season. If you are not making waves, you are treading water. Let’s look at how you can become the person everyone keeps talking about — for the right reasons.

____

## 1. **Commit to change**

If you simply do the same thing better than yesterday, guess what? You are on the fast track to Snoozeville. Embrace change and make it your best friend.

## 2. **What is remarkable to you?**

Ask yourself: would other people get excited about this? If the answer is no, you are merely fitting in. Aim for astonishing, not merely okay.

## 3. **Stand out for the right reasons**

Running down the street? Sure, it is noticeable, but let’s aim for impact, not just shock. Become the subject of conversation because you did something genuinely impressive.

## 4. **Extreme? Maybe. Memorable? Absolutely.**

Have you noticed that rock stars have fans in abundance? It is because they operate at the top of their craft. Reach for the stars — literally.

## 5. **Live on the edge**

Whether you are the fastest, richest, or even the slowest, it is all about the edge. Find your advantage and use it.

## 6. **Trying to please everyone? Forget it!**

You cannot make everyone happy, and that is okay. Focus on the people who understand you and share your vision. They are your real audience.

## 7. **Leave the rulebook behind**

If it has already been done, it is boring. Aim to be a pioneer. Remember Roger Bannister? Be like him, not the person who came next.

## 8. **Fear? What fear?**

It may feel frightening to step outside the lines, but that is where the magic happens. Be the exception, not the rule.

## 9. **T-shirt worthy?**

If people would not wear your idea on a T-shirt, it may not be as cool as you think. Go for passion, not passive interest.

## 10. **Stay ahead of the curve**

What is hot today may not be hot tomorrow. Keep evolving and keep being remarkable.

____

That is it, folks! Be brave, be different, and above all, be yourself — but the ultra-cool version. Go make some waves!
